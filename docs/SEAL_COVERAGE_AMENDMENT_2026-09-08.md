## The disagreement being resolved

The original mission forbids an automatic whole-session final pass or Final BAM.
`THE_ENGINE_CONTRACT.md` repeats that prohibition. Before this amendment,
`STT_CONTRACT.md` described an automatic retained-PCM whole-session comparison
and terminal gap repair. The in-process provider could also replace overlapping
decoder segments with one request-wide segment. Those were actual runtime and
documentation states, not evidence that the mission had already approved them.

## Current rule

Acoustic recovery of the take's own PCM is allowed before terminal finality.
It is an observation of uncommitted physical speech, never a second document
author. The normal stop path requests each material uncovered range directly;
it does not run an automatic whole-document comparison or replacement pass.

- The recorder hands off its finalized native mono i16 WAV with session,
  capture epoch, sample rate and captured sample count. The worker validates
  all of them. Sample indices match live f32 PCM; amplitudes are quantized.
  Live retention remains 120 seconds. Capture callbacks perform no archive
  reads or inference.
- Apple previews remain live. Timed candidates wait for the physical Silero
  interval to close before occurrence admission. Equal words on different PCM
  stay distinct; missing or ambiguous timing is not a synthetic occurrence.
- A gap provider receives exactly the uncovered PCM. Only its source-mapped
  segments wholly contained in that gap may seek calibrated qualification and
  observation admission through the unchanged `AcousticLedger` corridor.
  Invalid timing never becomes one whole-request witness. Coarse, missing,
  overlapping or straddling evidence remains a refusal.
- Local tail arming and the provider selector are frozen into the runtime
  settings snapshot. Off and invalid phase overrides remain explicit degraded
  states. The same arming decision feeds microphone capture and replay.
- Closure settles live Apple/Whisper observers, admits qualified gap evidence,
  drains every formatter slot created by that evidence, reassesses coverage,
  and only then requests the terminal seal. Light+, projection and delivery
  retain their existing downstream owners.
- The maximum uncovered-range tolerance remains **250 ms**. No producer,
  formatter, comparison string or archive receipt may waive that gate. The
  archive is audio provenance, not an occurrence or document seal.

The automatic formatter remains one occurrence-bound observer. An explicit
human Format command remains a separate existing revision action. No new
whole-document automatic formatter, ledger bypass or competing reducer exists.
